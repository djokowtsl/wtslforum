# Captured smash motion credit

Two Tennis-MoCap smash captures are retained:

- `tennis-smash.bvh` is the older Adorozco recording and remains unchanged. It is
  retained for provenance but is no longer selected for overheads.
- `tennis-smash-jarua.bvh` is the Jarua recording used for rally overheads.
  Its source file is `jarua_Remate.bvh`.

Both source files are from the Tennis-MoCap dataset:

- Dataset: [Tennis-MoCap](https://github.com/jdpulgarin/Tennis-MoCap)
- License: [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)

The Adorozco capture is by contributor `adorozco`; the selected contact is
inferred from a wrist-speed peak because its source has no ball-contact marker.
The Jarua capture is resampled to 25 Hz, with contact hand-picked at 7.6 s. Both
are retargeted to the WTSL player rig and distributed under CC BY-SA 3.0.
