TE4 Voice RoomTalk to your opponent or a group while you play. Audio goes straight between browsers.
Anyone with the code can join, so use a random one for private talk.
Settings
Most connections work with the built-in STUN servers. If two players cannot hear each other (strict routers or mobile networks), a TURN relay is needed.
Tips: use headphones to avoid echo. Voice is peer to peer, so players in the room can see each other's IP address (a normal WebRTC trait). Rooms hold up to 8 people.
