name: Character shot-choice guidance
description: Keep character profile shot recommendations useful without repeating them across every section.
---

For WTSL Forum character profiles, keep the copy concise and conversational. State the useful character detail directly; do not repeatedly explain that claims come from WTSL or the TE4 manual. Keep source attribution in one compact reference, not in the summary and every guidance card. Include shot-choice suggestions sparingly in a few relevant places rather than throughout summaries, comparisons, and every guidance card.

**Why:** The user said the source-heavy copy reads like an encyclopedia and preferred the earlier cadence. They also clarified that average maximal serve speed and average first-serve percentage are separate metrics, and that Safe’s 100% rate is the second-serve rule rather than a serve recommendation.

**How to apply:** Write summaries as natural descriptions of the character’s strengths without source or methodology caveats. Keep one concise TE4 reference near the mechanics section; avoid repeating “WTSL” or “the manual says” in profile copy. Preserve useful numbers and short, conditional shot cues. Label average maximal serve speed explicitly as a speed in km/h, and never conflate it with first-serve success. Use 70% Strong and 85% Normal as equal-rating baselines; when Power and Consistency differ, present labelled directional estimates for first-serve success with Strong and Normal. Treat Safe’s 100% as the second-serve rule, not a recommendation. Keep Precision separate from the success estimate unless a source confirms its effect. Treat narrow model estimates (such as a shoulder-height fit) as that scenario only; never turn them into universal advice or repeat the same incoming-ball case across multiple cards. In Shot Variety, prioritize Drop Shot and Slice Mastery; only discuss Lob when it stands out on the roster. Banana-shot, short-slice, and defensive-slice recommendations must remain conditional profile heuristics, not claims guaranteed by the manual. Describe the actual stroke setup in plain language and explain documented mechanics; compare the character’s displayed Forehand and Backhand ratings, but never compare hypothetical alternate builds. Preserve every character-specific game-plan detail, its original section labels and order; do not rewrite, deduplicate, or cap the plan. Remove only weight references and redundant source attribution, and preserve all rally-fit analyses.

**Why:** The user noticed that shortening the game plan removed useful character-specific information and asked to keep the fuller content visible.

### Court fit and physical profile guidance

For character profiles, 50% Topspin is neutral; values close to 50% are near-neutral, and surface effects grow with distance from that midpoint. Explain slow, medium-paced, and fast-court fit without claiming that 51% meaningfully favors slow courts. Tiredness is always on for all characters; explain fatigue using Speed, Muscle Tone, and Stamina. Weight does not affect gameplay and must not appear in profile copy.

**Why:** The user clarified the always-on Tiredness setting and lack of weight gameplay effect, and asked for medium-court guidance, near-neutral copy at 51% Topspin, physical comparisons, restored rally-fit analyses, and character-specific tactical plans.

**How to apply:** Compare Speed, Muscle Tone, and Stamina with same-tour roster percentiles; explain that Speed sets top running speed, Speed and Muscle Tone average into running acceleration, and Stamina relative to that average affects running fatigue. State directly that Tiredness is enabled for all characters and that running and strokes drain energy; do not use conditional on/off wording. Omit weight from headings, dimensions, mechanics, and general profile guidance.
