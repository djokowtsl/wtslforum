---
name: DMO replay public publication
description: Public visibility rules for submitted match replay files.
---

Public visitors may list and play only uploaded DMO files with submitter public-sharing consent or an active, explicit admin publication override. All other submissions remain available only to signed-in members. Admin overrides record the authorizer and reason and can be revoked without deleting the stored file. TE4 Coach sharing retains its separate consent rule.

**Why:** The owner requested public visitor access while explicitly keeping unapproved submissions private.

**How to apply:** Keep guest listing, metadata, and playback authorization on the same consent-or-override gate; preserve private Blob storage and the separate TE4 Coach consent check.
